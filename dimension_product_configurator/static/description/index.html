Dimension Product
Configurator

Sell products by dimensions. Your salesperson enters height, width and depth on the quotation, and the price is calculated instantly — per m², per m³, per kg or per linear meter.

The problem it solves

If every item you sell has a different size, a standard price list is useless. Most companies end up quoting from a spreadsheet, then retyping the result into Odoo — slow, and one transcription error away from selling at a loss. This module puts the calculation inside the quotation itself.

📏

Made to measure

Every unit has its own size, and the price follows automatically.

🔢

Five formulas

Area, volume, weight, length or fixed price — chosen per product.

⚙️

Your own vocabulary

You name the parameters. Length, height, thickness, depth — your call.

See it in action

One quotation, three trades

A window priced per m² in centimeters, a countertop per m³ in millimeters, a steel sheet per kg. Each line carries its configuration summary and its computed area, volume or weight.

The configurator, opened from the order line

Dimensions, options, and a full price breakdown that updates as you type. Your salesperson can justify every line to the customer.

Pick the formula, not the code

Five pricing modes, the dimension unit your team actually works in, and the resulting formula written out with your own parameter names.

Setup lives in a single tab

One line per parameter. The "Role in the formula" column designates the dimensions x, y, z and the material. Anything without a role is simply an option.

Each material carries its own rate

Rates are expressed in the unit of the selected mode. Density is only asked for when you price by weight.

Options and surcharges

Selection lists and yes/no options, each with its own surcharge and its default value. No formula editor, no scripting.

Five pricing modes

Mode Formula Typical use
Per m² x × y × rate Windows, glazing, flooring, banners
Per m³ x × y × z × rate Solid timber, concrete, insulation, foam
Per kg x × y × z × density × rate Sheet metal, steel, aluminium plate
Per linear meter x × rate Profiles, skirting, cable, tubing
Fixed price surcharges only Option-driven products

Dimensions are entered in the unit you choose — millimeters, centimeters or meters — and converted internally. A sheet metal shop works in mm, a joiner in cm, a flooring contractor in m, all on the same install.

How it works

1

Describe your product once

Pick a pricing mode, add your parameters, and tell the module which ones are the dimensions x, y, z and which one carries the material rate.

2

Set your rates

Each material carries its own rate, in the unit of the selected mode. Options add fixed surcharges. No code, no formula editor.

3

Configure from the quotation

Add the product to a sales order and open the configurator. Enter the dimensions, pick the options, and see the price breakdown update as you type.

4

The quotation updates itself

Unit price, computed area, volume or weight, and a readable configuration summary are written back onto the order line and printed on the PDF.
